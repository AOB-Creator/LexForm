# Services, works (pudrat), GPH, staffing, joint activity

Legal base: Civil Code RUz — paid services (ҳақ эвазига хизмат кўрсатиш / возмездное оказание услуг), contract
work (пудрат / подряд incl. construction pudrat), simple partnership (оддий ширкат / простое товарищество); Law
"On contractual-legal basis of business entities" — typical penalties: supplier/executor delay 0.5%/day ≤ 50% of
undelivered; buyer/customer late payment 0.4%/day ≤ 50% of overdue amount.
Budget-funded customers: contract enters into force only after **registration with Treasury (Ғазначилик)**.

· 3 Price (digits & words) by transfer to executor's account · 4 Liability (executor delay 0.5%/day ≤ 50% of
unrendered; customer late payment 0.4%/day ≤ 50%; civil law otherwise) · 5 Force majeure (war, unrest, epidemic,
embargo, earthquake, flood, fire; notify; >3 months continuous → terminate by written notice) · 6 Disputes
(negotiation → pre-trial procedure → **Economic court**) · 7 Amendments in writing, valid after Treasury
registration; early termination by agreement or per Civil Code with damages · 8 Final (2 copies; annex)
· Requisites: address, phone (+99898…), settlement account (Х.р), bank, МФО, СТИР, ХХТУТ (OKED); head's signature,
М.Ў.

## Договор-ГПХ-на-оказание-услуг и АКТ (doc, Russian; 3 copies) — civil-law services contract with individual
"ДОГОВОР НА ОКАЗАНИЕ УСЛУГ": Customer (organization or IE, acting under charter/POA/registration certificate) ↔
Executor = **individual** (FIO, passport, address).
1 Subject (services list; period from–to, executor schedules intervals but notifies customer; may finish early;
rendered on signing acceptance act) · 2 Rights & duties (quality; full & timely; fix defects free within N days;
may engage third parties with written consent; customer provides conditions — staff presence, information,
database access; accepts by act & pays within N days; may inspect; may refuse pro-rata) · 3 Price = remuneration
+ expenses (digits & words, сум) · 4 Liability (X%/day of contract sum for executor delay and customer late
payment; civil law; penalty doesn't release) · 5 Other (negotiation → pre-trial → court at customer's location;
written amendments; annexes integral; 2 copies) · 6 Requisites & signatures.
Attached **"Акт выполненных работ"** to contract №/date: executor rendered services …; amount (digits & words) сум
**without VAT**; no quality claims; signatures.

## пудрат шартнома (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies, partly filled) — current repair works contract
"ПУДРАТ ШАРТНОМАСИ №", Andijan. Customer (Буюртмачи, head acting under Низом) ↔ Contractor (Пудратчи LLC).
I Subject (current repair of object; customer creates conditions, accepts, pays) · II Price incl. all taxes, in
current prices, **excl. VAT** — fixed except force majeure or customer's change of scope (by supplementary
agreement) · III Contractor duties (perform per design & estimate docs, in scope & terms; safety; full property
liability for proper completion & commissioning) · IV Customer duties (hand over site & design-estimate docs
within 3 days by act; architectural-construction supervision; accept work; advance & financing per schedule)
· V Terms (start from first advance; duration N days per tender; valid until year) · VI Payments (**50% advance**;
50% within 3 bank days after act of completed works; if customer fails obligations within 10 calendar days,
contractor may amend or terminate unilaterally, still paid for work done) · VII Execution (customer appoints
**technical auditor**; contractor keeps site clean, removes debris) · VIII Force majeure (2 months without
agreement → terminate) · IX Acceptance of completed object per rules; ownership passes on acceptance · X Warranties
(scope, quality per norms, materials, fix defects in warranty period; bilateral defect acts) · XI Termination
(customer: start delayed >1 month; completion delayed >1 month; quality breaches; contractor: works suspended by
customer >1 month; financing not met; unfinished construction handed over within a month and paid; written
notice; guilty party pays damages incl. lost profit; no unilateral termination otherwise) · XII Liability
(customer late financing 0.1%/day ≤ 5%; contractor late commissioning 0.1%/day ≤ 5% of contract value; late
defect fixing 0.1%/day ≤ 5% of defective works; customer may set off penalties from payments) · XIII Disputes →
economic court · XIV Special (entire agreement; written amendments; 2 copies) · XV Requisites (х/р, bank code/МФО,
ИНН, bank branch, ХТУТ).

## ПУДРАТ ИШЛАРИ ШАРТНОМАСИ (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies, filled examples ×2) — general ↔ sub-contractor
"КАПИТАЛ ТАЪМИРЛАШ ИШЛАРИ ШАРТНОМАСИ" №22 (2016) and №14 (2017), Andijan. General contractor (Бош пудратчи) ↔
Sub-contractor (Ёрдамчи пудратчи), both LLCs under Низом. Sub-contractor performs at own risk for the stated price
capital construction / capital & current repair per customer's assignment and design docs (e.g. 10 model houses;
maternity hospital reconstruction).
III Contractor: work per design, **КМК and ШНК** (construction norms), calendar plan; hand over by act; fix defects
at own cost; inform about engaged specialized subcontractors; safety, fire safety, guarding customer's materials
· IV Customer: hand over site by act; **15% advance** before start; accept monthly & pay in full by 10th of each
month; supply materials per progress; provide design-estimate docs; accept object within 10 days of written notice
· V Price (e.g. 984 480 000 / 1 464 000 000 сум in words) may change with inflation; monthly acceptance with act
and **forms Ф-2, Ф-3** (completed works act / cost certificate) · VI Amendments on change of material prices,
minimum wage, wages, equipment (written, by agreement); termination on serious breach with 1 month notice
· VII Emergencies (no warranty liability for misuse or accidents; force majeure) · VIII Late payment 0.1%/day
(capped at 0.4% of total) · IX Disputes → economic/arbitration court · X Legal addresses & bank details (bank
branch, х/р, ИНН, МФО).
⚠ Party labels in the requisites block are swapped relative to the preamble.

## 19СОВМЕСТНАЯ ДЕЯТЕЛЬНОСТЬ Договор шаблон (docx, Russian; 2 copies) — simple partnership (3 participants)
"ДОГОВОР ПРОСТОГО ТОВАРИЩЕСТВА (о совместной деятельности) N". Participants 1–3 (via representative acting on
basis of …).
1 Subject & goals (combine contributions, joint business without forming a legal entity) · 2 Contributions (what,
value, order & terms per participant) · 3 Common property = **common shared ownership**; shares per participant;
no disposal of share without consent; income distribution order & terms · 4 Conduct of common affairs entrusted to
Participant N with listed powers; acts on behalf of all within goals; prepares documents (contracts, invoices),
keeps accounting & reporting, distributes income; opens a **secondary on-demand deposit account** for all joint
settlements within N days; others may monitor & request documents; reports with specified content/term
· 5 Costs & losses covered from common property, then in agreed proportions; taxes paid by each party
separately (except customs, state duty, fees) · 6 Liability (fine + damages for refusal outside 8.3; managing
participant liable for reporting/accounting breaches) · 7 Force majeure · 8 Indefinite term; termination cases;
unilateral withdrawal only in listed cases with written notice, effective after N; contract continues for others;
contributed property returned to the leaving party · 9 Other (claims procedure → economic court or arbitration;
effective on signing; simple written amendments; 3 originals, language N) · 10 Requisites (ИНН, ОКОНХ, legal
address, phone/fax, account, bank, МФО; signatures, М.П.).
