# Lease contracts (Ижара шартномалари / Договоры аренды)

Legal base: Civil Code of RUz ch. on lease (arts. ~535–600; housing lease art. 606, 612 — housing lease ≤ 5 years,
default 5 years if no term), Law "On Lease" (Ижара тўғрисида, art. 11 — inseparable improvements without consent
pass to lessor free), Law "On the contractual-legal basis of business entities' activity", GC arts. 551–552
(termination). Real-estate leases must be **registered with the State Tax Service within 10 days** (see below).
Lessee withholding: when lessor is an individual and lessee a legal entity, lessee withholds personal income tax.

written consent · 3 Term until date from signing of Transfer Act; pre-emptive renewal right, notice ≥1 month
· 4 Transfer by Transfer Act (Annex 2) · 5 Payments: rent X сум/month to lessor's account; accrues from act
until actual return; excludes utilities (electricity, cold/hot water); 100% prepayment 25th–30th of previous
month; **PIT withheld** by lessee; utilities invoice by 25th with receipts, paid in 5 bank days · 6 Rights &
duties (lessor: hand over fit premises; not obstruct; notify of requisite changes in 3 working days; fire/sanitary
compliance; urgent repairs for floods/pipe bursts. Lessee: use per purpose; current repairs & accident response
at own cost; pay; no inseparable alterations without consent; allow inspections; vacate at end; notify changes in
10 working days; may put up signage) · 7 Return within 5 working days by act, normal wear; inseparable improvements
pass to lessor uncompensated · 8 Liability: late rent penalty 1%/day ≤ 50% of monthly rent; late handover same;
disputes → Tashkent City Economic Court · 9 Force majeure (notify within 3 days) · 10 Termination: no unilateral
early termination except — lessor with 3 months' notice, or 30 days on material breach (non-payment w/o
explanation letter); lessee with 30 days' notice or on material breach (no repairs; handover delay >7 days);
evasion of signing acts; rent until actual return; penalties don't release from performance · 11 Final
(effective on signing; amendments within a month by supplementary agreements; no assignment without consent;
annexes; confidentiality with damages incl. lost profit; entire agreement; 2 copies) · 12 Requisites.
Annex 1 = plan; Annex 2 = ПЕРЕДАТОЧНЫЙ АКТ (premises at address, area; conforms, no claims).

## Ижара шартномаси намуналари (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies) — official regulation + model forms
"Кўчмас мулк ижара шартномаларини давлат солиқ хизмати органларида мажбурий ҳисобга қўйиш тартиби тўғрисида
НИЗОМ" (approved by State Tax Committee resolution №2018-65 of 15.08.2018; MoJ reg. №3077 of 11.10.2018; based on
Law "On State Tax Service" and Presidential Decree ПҚ-3741 of 25.05.2018). Key rules:
- Real-estate leases are concluded per Civil Code and **mandatorily registered with the tax service**; housing lease
  ≤ 5 years (GC art. 612). Individuals may use the model form (Annex 1) or free form.
- Lessor (or representative/trust manager) must register within **10 days** of signing — electronically via the
  tax-committee website personal cabinet (request form Annex 3 + contract + ID copy), or on paper at the tax office
  of residence/registration or property location. Registration is real-time/immediate; notification with **QR code**
  (Annex 4) links to the XML of the registered contract. Refusal if documents non-compliant.
- Monthly rent may not be below the statutory minimum.
- When both parties are individuals, a PIT payment notice for the current year is issued on registration.
- Termination: expires at end of term; court termination → lessor submits court decision; early termination by
  agreement → agreement (Annex 5 for individuals, or free form) registered via request (Annex 6), notification with
  QR (Annex 7).
Annex 1 — **Уй-жойни ижарага бериш шартномаси (model form)**: date/place; parties with permanent registration
address (region, district, МФЙ, street, house, apartment), birth year, FIO, passport. 1 Subject (address; total &
living area in digits and words, rooms; ownership basis — notarial office №, register № & date, title document:
sale/gift/exchange/inheritance certificate; term) · 2 Rent per year/month/day, total; payment day; unilateral
termination if rent late more than twice in a row · 3 Rights & duties (use per purpose, sanitary & fire rules;
utilities — water, electricity, gas, phone, waste — paid by lessee or lessor; lessor pays taxes on income)
· 4 Liability & disputes (amend/terminate by agreement, court; 1 month's written notice; lessee may terminate any
time with 3 months' notice with consent of co-residents) · 5 Final (Tax Code arts. 80, 176, 191, 192 explained;
co-residents per GC art. 606; lessor warrants property not transferred, pledged, disputed or prohibited; 3 copies —
one kept by tax authority). Signatures.
Annex 2 — Act of handover of household items (table: name, model, qty, condition, note) + meter readings
(electricity, gas, cold water: unit, reading, sealed, note).
Annexes 3–7 — registration request, QR notification (register №, lessor FIO/name, address, СТИР, registration date,
term), termination agreement, termination registration request, termination notification.
