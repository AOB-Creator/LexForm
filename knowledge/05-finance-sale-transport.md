# Finance, sale, transport, security & amendment documents

## Financial aid / loans (Молиявий ёрдам)
Interest-free inter-company loans ("financial aid", молиявий ёрдам / финансовая помощь) are common in Uzbekistan
between related LLCs. Civil Code loan rules apply; returnable financial aid is a loan; non-returnable is a gift-like
transfer (tax consequences — income for recipient).

### Шартнома молиявий ёрдам (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies) — interest-free credit
Lender ("Кредит берувчи", LLC head under Низом) ↔ Borrower ("Кредит олувчи"). 1 Amount (digits & words) lent for the
contract period · 2 Must be returned by end of term; **no interest**; valid from–to · 3 Lender transfers after
signing & sealing, "according to its capacity"; borrower repays in full on time; on default lender may recover by
**payment demand (тўлов талабномаси) without acceptance** from borrower's account · 4 Amendments by consent;
disputes → economic court; 2 copies · Addresses (х/р, МФО, ИНН, ОКОНХ, phone, head, М.Ў.).

### Молиявий ёрдам шартномаси (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies, three filled examples) — financial aid, LLC ↔ LLC
"ШАРТНОМА № … Молиявий ёрдам кўрсатиш тўғрисида" (Fergana, 2021). Lender ("Қарз берувчи") / Borrower ("Қарз
олувчи"), directors. I Purpose (lender gives financial loan; borrower returns on time) · II Lender transfers in one
or several tranches to borrower's bank account; **terms confidential** · III Amount · IV Return by 31.12 of the year;
effective on signing until funds fully transferred; 2 copies · V Disputes per RUz law · Requisites (address, bank
branch, х/р, МФО, ИНН, **ОКЭД**, phone, head). The three examples show circular lending between related companies.

### ДОГОВОР безв.фин.помощи (doc, Russian; 3 identical copies) — non-repayable financial aid
"Договор № предоставления безвозвратной финансовой помощи", Tashkent. Party-1 ↔ Party-2 (directors, charter).
1 General (Party-1 transfers money into Party-2's ownership; no return, no compensation) · 2 Amount (digits &
words, сум) · 3 One payment within 10 working days by payment order to Party-2's account; deemed transferred on
crediting · 4 Disputes (negotiations → court); no liability without fault · 5 Effective on signing, ends on
crediting; amendments/termination by supplementary agreement · 6 Final (supplements valid if written, signed,
sealed; 2 copies) · Requisites & signatures.

## Security & debt transfer

### ДОГОВОР ПОРУЧИТЕЛЬСТВА N (docx, Russian; 2 copies) — suretyship (bilateral)
Surety (Поручитель) ↔ Creditor (Кредитор, also by letter №/date). I Basis (secures obligation of Debtor under
contract №/date; subject of that contract) · II Subject (surety answers for Debtor's obligation; on Debtor's default,
on Creditor's demand, surety performs listed actions & bears listed liability; scope; term from demand) · III
Transfer of creditor's rights to surety upon performance (rights, documents to hand over, term; surety notifies
Debtor) · IV Term · V Liability (Creditor may demand from surety **without first demanding from Debtor** — joint
liability; damages for unjustified refusal by either side) · VI Force majeure (notify within 5 days) · VII
Termination of suretyship (debtor performs; term expiry; amendments increasing surety's burden without consent;
debt transfer to new debtor without surety's consent; creditor refuses proper performance; other) · VIII Justified
refusal grounds · IX Final (RUz law; negotiations/claims → court or arbitration; 3 originals in Russian — third copy
to the Debtor) · X Requisites.

### Договор о переводе долга / Договор уступки (doc/docx, Russian; 2 near-identical, 3 copies) — debt transfer
"ДОГОВОР № Уступки права требования и перевода долга", Tashkent, tripartite: Debtor, New Debtor (LLC), Creditor
(each via director under Charter), based on **Civil Code ch. 23, arts. 313, 315, 316, 320–323**.
1 Subject (Debtor, by agreement with New Debtor and **with Creditor's consent**, transfers **partially** its debt to
Creditor under contract №/date for amount (digits & words) per **reconciliation act** (акт сверки) of date;
documents are annexes) · 2 Debtor hands over documents confirming the debt; Debtor becomes indebted to New Debtor
for the transferred amount · 3 Damages for improper performance · 4 Effective on signing until full performance;
amendments; severability; 3 copies · 5 Disputes (negotiations → Economic courts of RUz; older version says
"Хозяйственные суды") · Requisites of New Debtor, Debtor, Creditor.
Note: the title says "assignment of claim and debt transfer" but the body only implements a debt transfer.

## Sale of goods & property (Олди-сотди / Купля-продажа)

### олди-сотди шартнома (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies, partly filled) — general goods sale
"Олди-сотди шартномаси (умумий)", Tashkent. Seller (private enterprise ХК, director, charter) ↔ Buyer (LLC).
1 Subject · 2 Goods table (№, name, unit, qty, price, total) + total in digits & words; **≥15% advance within 5
days**; 85% within 10 days after acceptance & **invoice (хисоб-фактура)** · 3 Seller delivers within 20 days of
advance (⚠ text says "Харидор" — drafting error); buyer pays on time · 4 Quality per applicable standards · 5
Liability: seller delay/short delivery **0.5%/day ≤ 50%** of undelivered; buyer late payment **0.4%/day ≤ 50%**;
otherwise Civil Code + Law №670-I of 29.08.1998 "On contractual-legal basis of business entities' activity"
· 6 Disputes (negotiation → court) · 7 Force majeure (earthquake, flood, riots, strikes…; written notice; terms
extended; no notice → loses right to invoke) · 8 Effective on signing until full performance · 9 Written
amendments; notify of requisite/name changes within 10 days; 2 copies · 10 Requisites & signatures.

### Олди сотди шартномаси намунаси (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies) — goods sale (supply) sample
Margilan, 2020. Seller (private enterprise, head under Низом) ↔ Buyer (joint venture, General Director under charter).
1 Subject + goods table №1 + total · 2 **100% prepayment within 10 bank days**; bank transfer · 3 Seller: deliver
within 20 bank days of payment with documents, adequate packaging; Buyer: inspect within 5 days and send
**claim (рекламация)**; seller replaces defective goods within 20 days · 4 Liability: delay 0.5%/day ≤ 50%;
non-conforming quality/assortment/grade → buyer may refuse & claim **20% fine** of their value (collected
without acceptance) or refund; buyer late payment 0.01%/day ≤ 50%; per contractual-basis Law · 5 Written amendments;
early termination by agreement or law with damages · 6 Disputes: mandatory written **claim procedure**, 1-month
response period, then lawsuit at defendant's economic court · 7 2 copies; term until 31 Dec; extra conditions
· 8 Requisites.

### Договор купли продажи помещ (docx, Russian; 2 copies) — sale of non-residential premises
"ДОГОВОР № купли-продажи нежилого помещения". Seller ↔ Buyer (entities via representatives).
1 Subject (premises: cadastral №, area in digits & words, address, floor/storeys, share in common ownership of the
building; technical passport, plans, schemes, explications handed over; ownership certificate; inventory value per
certificate №/date/issuer; seller warrants not sold, pledged, disputed, arrested, free of third-party rights; land
rights transfer order; capital repair date) · 2 Price (final, fixed); **buyer bears state-registration costs**;
payment within N bank days · 3 Effective until registration of title & settlement · 4 Handover within N days by
acceptance act signed & sealed · 5 **Title passes on state registration** in the Unified State Register of Rights
**and** payment; risk of accidental loss on seller until then · 6 Duties (seller: transfer without encumbrance,
documents for registration & land use, no purpose restrictions, warrant document accuracy; buyer: pay, accept,
submit registration docs) · 7 Liability = actual direct damage · 8 Disputes → "arbitration court" (⚠ RF term; in
RUz → economic court) · 9 Written amendments; non-payment → termination & return of premises; **3 copies** (third
for registration) · Requisites, М.П.

## Transport / carriage (Юк ташиш)

### Юк ташиш шартномаси пустой (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies, carrier pre-filled) — road cargo carriage
"Автомобил транспортида юк ташиш учун тузилган №-сонли ШАРТНОМА", Namangan 2017. Customer (Буюртмачи) ↔ Carrier
(Ташувчи LLC, director under Низом).
1 Subject (deliver cargo to destination; customer pays per cl. 5.1 prices) · 2 Conditions (**waybill (йўл варақаси)**
sealed by carrier gives driver right to load/unload/enter; no illegal, ownerless, explosive cargo or narcotics) ·
3 Carrier (sanitary & technically sound vehicles; safety, traffic & fire rules; choose vehicle type/count; deliver
to consignee's responsible person on time; instruct drivers; use customer's funds for required license) · 4 Customer
(timely waybills, **consignment notes (юк хатлари)**, certificates, specifications; record actual arrival times;
liable for errors in consignment notes; no undocumented cargo; continuous loading/unloading within norms; load per
vehicle capacity; covers for bulk/polluting cargo; fix loading point; responsible person at loading/unloading;
mechanisms; night lighting; passable access roads; driver amenities — food, lodging, parking, guarding — when away
from base) · 5 Settlements (tariffs per **price-agreement protocol** annex; total sum; by invoice & bank transfer)
· 6 Liability (carrier liable for loss/shortage/damage unless proves no fault; shortage act on the spot; idle
return of vehicle → hourly tariff; damage deducted from freight by act; disputes → Namangan Regional Economic Court)
· 7 Special (price/law changes → agree new price within 5 days or terminate; written amendments) · 8 Force majeure
(per **CabMin Resolution №63 of 15.02.2005** on confirming force majeure) · 9 Term (to 31 Dec), 2 copies
· Requisites (address, phone, х/р, bank, МФО, ИНН, ОКОНХ, ОКЭД).
Annex: **Protocol (баённома) of free prices** for road cargo — table (service, loading/unloading points, unit, price
excl. VAT, note); prices revised on growth of prices, wages, fuel with written notice.

### ТРАНС_ДА_ЮК_ТАШИШ_УЧУН_ШАРТНОМА (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies) — short carriage contract
Navoi, 200_. Shipper (Юк жўнатувчи) ↔ Carrier (Юк ташувчи). Carrier delivers per requests; volume (tonnes),
turnover (t·km), sum; shipper pays per prevailing prices; shipper prepares cargo and documents incl. **commodity-
transport document (ТТН / товар-транспорт накладной)** & certificates; carrier allocates vehicles; monthly invoice
on completed TTNs; **50% prepayment** subject to financing, rest within 20 days after funds received; 0.5% penalty on
unpaid part; disputes by agreement or law; validity from–to; addresses.

## Amendments (Дополнительные соглашения)

"Приложение № к Договору №…; Дополнительное Соглашение № к Договору № от …", city/district, date. Parties as
LLC/JSC/PE ("ООО/АО/ЧП") — Executor/Seller and Customer/Buyer (acting under Charter/POA). 1 Amend cl. 5.1 — contract
sum (digits & words) **without VAT**; 2 Amend cl. 9.1 — validity until date; 3 other clauses remain in force;
4 effective on signing; 5 two copies. Signatures (FIO, position).
