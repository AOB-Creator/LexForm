## Бухгалтерия_Ҳисоб_сиёсати_намунаси (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic) — accounting policy model (LLC, 2020)
Approved annually by director's order (NAS №1 "Accounting policy and financial statements", item 4); drafted by
chief accountant, submitted in December, applied from 1 January; no mid-year changes (NAS №1 item 56) except
reorganization, change of founders, change of law/regulation, new methods; changes by director's order with
monetary effect disclosed in explanatory note.
Legal base: Law "On Accounting" (№ЗРУ-404 of 13.04.2016), NAS (БҲМС), Tax Code, Regulation on cost composition
(CabMin №54 of 05.02.1999).
Content: 1 General (company info, OKED, signature rights: 1st — director, 2nd — chief accountant) · 2 Principles &
organization (head responsible per Law art. 11; centralized accounting department; software, journal-order form;
document flow per MinFin order №1297 of 14.01.2004) · 3 Financial statements: Form 1 balance sheet (NAS №15),
Form 2 income statement (NAS №3), due by **15 February**; users: owners, tax, statistics; explanatory notes order
· 4 Accounting: fixed assets (NAS №5; capitalization threshold > **50× base calculation amount**, useful life > 1 yr;
account 08 → 01; borrowing interest not capitalized; bank fees & contract costs expensed; FX at CBU rate;
**straight-line depreciation**; disposal per reg. №1401; annual revaluation on 1 Jan by indexation (reg. №1192);
annual inventory per NAS №19; repairs expensed) · intangibles (NAS №7; straight-line; 5 years if life unknown;
goodwill ≤ 20 years; account 05xx) · inventory (NAS №4; acquisition cost excl. recoverable VAT; contributions at
agreed value; donated at market value; perpetual system; **FIFO**) · goods (FIFO; storage/sorting costs to selling
expenses) · deferred expenses (property-complex premium written off over 24 months) · cash (excess over limit
deposited within 3 days; PKO/RKO cash orders; bound & sealed cash book; monthly cash count) · FX (CBU rate;
monthly revaluation) · receivables (account 40, e-invoices) · corporate cards (account 5532; report within 10 days;
misuse withheld from salary per LC art. 164) · payables (by type & currency) · payroll (time-based) · founders
(profit distribution by founder's decision) · equity & retained earnings (NAS №3) · revenue (NAS №2, recognized when
service rendered) · cost (simple method) · period expenses · FX differences · off-balance account 014 (inventory in
use) · 5 Job descriptions of accounting staff · 6 Working chart of accounts per NAS №21.
