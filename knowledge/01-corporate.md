# Corporate / founder documents (Таъсисчилик, МЧЖ / ООО)

Context: Uzbek LLC (МЧЖ — масъулияти чекланган жамият; MChJ; ООО). Governing law: Law of RUz
"Масъулияти чекланган ҳамда қўшимча масъулиятли жамиятлар тўғрисида" (LLC Law), Civil Code (ФК),
Labour Code (Меҳнат кодекси). Registration is done at the Davlat xizmatlari markazi / Ягона дарча
(One-Stop-Shop) of the district.

Common pattern: a **Қарор** (decision of the sole participant / protocol of the general meeting)
is almost always paired with a **Буйруқ** (director's order) that executes it, with "Асос:" (basis)
referencing the decision number and date.

## МЧЖ ташкил этиш ягона иштирокчи қарори ва директор буйруғи (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic)
Decision №1 of the sole participant to establish an LLC + Order №1 of director taking office.
- Fields: LLC name, date, city/district, participant full name, charter fund amount (digits + words, сум),
  registration center (давлат хизматлари маркази), director full name, birth year, passport (series/number,
  issuing ИИБ district, date).
- Decision items: 1) establish LLC; 2) approve charter fund; 3) register at state services center;
  4) appoint director.
- Order №1: director assumes office from date; takes right of first signature, seal and corner stamp;
  salary per staffing table. Basis: employment contract, registration certificate, decision №1.

## Решение_учредителя_назнач_Директора (docx, Russian)
"РЕШЕНИЕ № 1 единственного учредителя ООО" — appoints a Director.
- Fields: city, date, founder FIO + passport (series AB/AA, issued by ИИБ, date) + registered address;
  director FIO + passport + address.

## Қарор №5 Директор тайинлаш (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic)
Protocol (БАЁНИ) №5 of the founders' general meeting (multi-founder LLC).
- Header: region/city, LLC name, chairman (раис), secretary (котиб), attendees without vote.
- Agenda: 1) release current director; 2) appoint new director.
- "Эшитилди" (heard) narrative → "ҚАРОР ҚИЛАДИ": release from date; appoint from date; grant first
  signature right; execution entrusted to director. Signed by chairman and secretary.

## Карор Буйрук директор (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic, filled example)
Decision №1 "Директорлик масъулияти тўғрисида" + Order №1 "Кадрлар таркибига оид — Ишга қабул қилиш".
Founder self-appoints as director with first & second signature right, round seal and corner stamp.
Basis: founder's decision/protocol of the same date.

## Директор_вазифасига_киришиш_буйруғи (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic, sample)
Order "Жамият директори вазифасига киришиш тўғрисида". Legal basis cited: Labour Code arts. 77–87,
LLC Law art. 39. Items: director took office from date per founders' meeting decision №; salary per staffing
table. Basis: founders' meeting decision + employment contract with the founder (number/date).

## Korxona nomi o'zgarishi qaror (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic, filled example)
Sole founder decision №01 to rename LLC ("MOON MASSAJ KLINIKASI" → "PROSPERA TRADE", Nukus, 27.06.2024)
+ Order №01 executing it.
- Items: 1) approve amendments to Charter; 2) change name; 3) register amendments at district Ягона дарча.

## Yuridik manzil o'zgarishi QAROR 01 (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic, filled example)
Sole founder decision №01 to change legal address (old → new full address) + Order №01.
- Items: 1) approve amendments to Founding agreement (Таъсис шартномаси) and Charter; 2) change legal
  address; 3) register amendments at district Ягона дарча.
- Founder identified by FIO, birth date, passport series/number, issuing ИИБ, date.

## Устав (yangi tahrir) (docx, Uzbek Latin)
Full LLC Charter, new edition, approved by sole participant's decision. Sections:
I Umumiy qoidalar (prior registration: DXM, date, reestr №; participants and shares %; full/short firm name
in Uzbek & Russian; postal address; unlimited term) · II Legal status (participants liable within contributions;
unpaid-share solidary liability) · III Branches/representative offices · IV Purpose & activity (profit; OKED
activity text; licensed activities after license) · V Participants' rights/obligations (manage, info, profit
share, sell share, exit any time, ≥10% may sue to expel participant) · VI Property · VII Charter fund (amount;
shares; fully paid within 1 year of registration; increase only after fully paid; decrease → notify creditors
within 30 days + publish in media; effective for third parties after registration) · VIII Exit of participant
(actual value of share returned in kind or money) · IX Transfer of share (only paid part; simple written form)
· X Document storage list · XI Accounting & reporting · XII Reserve fund 15% of charter fund · XIII Governing
bodies (General meeting = supreme; Director = executive; optional supervisory board/audit commission)
· XIV General meeting (votes ∝ share; exclusive competences a–n; a, b, v, z, m require unanimity, rest by
majority) · XV Director (elected by GM; must be RUz citizen; employment contract signed by GM chair; acts
without POA; issues POAs; hires/fires; orders; large deals by GM decision) · XVI Control (participants or
independent auditor) · XVII Employees (collective agreement) · XVIII Trade secret · XIX Reorganization &
liquidation (Civil Code arts. 49–55; LLC Law arts. 49–55) · XX Disputes (negotiation, then court)
· XXI Final provisions. Signature block: participants with share amount and %.

## Устав изменен (docx, Russian) — ⚠ misnamed
Not a charter. It's a Russian web article (patent-rus.ru) on contributing intellectual property to charter

## устав купайтири баён орг (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic, filled example; 3 copies, dated 2017 & 2023)
Protocol (БАЁННОМАСИ) №3 of a private enterprise (хусусий корхона) meeting — **increase of charter fund**
by founder's in-kind contribution of inventory (товар-моддий бойликлар) worth 174 485 000 сўм.
- Structure: city/date; attendance (100%); agenda; speeches (founder proposes); unanimous vote;
  "ҚАРОР ҚИЛАДИ": 1) contribute goods to charter fund; 2) formalize transfer with an act (далолатнома) and
  reflect in accounting. Signed by chairman and members.
- Note: the 2023 copy has inconsistent names ("KORXONA" vs "Jamshidjon Universal Savdo") — leftover from
  editing.

## Устав_фонди_камайишини_расмийлаштирамиз (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic, explanatory article; 2 copies)
Q&A guidance on **reducing an LLC charter fund** (LLC Law art. 19). Key rules:
- Mandatory reduction when: charter fund not fully paid within 1 year of registration (reduce to paid amount
  or liquidate); additional contributions not paid by deadline (art. 18); net assets < charter fund after 2nd
  and each subsequent financial year (reduce to net assets; if net assets < minimum = 40× minimum monthly wage
  → liquidate).
- Net assets = balance line 400 − line 490 − line 600 (NAS №14; MinFin order №140).
- Procedure: decision by protocol of participants' meeting; within 30 days notify all known creditors in
  writing + publish in mass media; creditors may demand early performance/damages within 30 days; registration
  of amendments only with proof of creditor notification (≤1 month after GM approval); inform tax authority
  within 10 days (Tax Code art. 83); re-registration within 30 days (CabMin resolution №66 of 09.02.2017,
  annex 7, sec. II items 6–7).
- Cannot reduce below minimum charter fund (40× MMW; then 5 991 000 сўм). Wholesale trade license required
  charter fund ≥ 3500× MMW in cash (CabMin №242 of 05.11.2005) — notify licensor.
- Accounting entries: Dt 8330 Kt 6620 (reduction, debt to founders); Dt 6620 Kt 5110 (cash return);
  Dt 6620 Kt 9210 (return as fixed asset); disposal entries 9210/0160, 0260/9210, 8510/9210, 9210/9310;
  reduction to net assets: Dt 6620 Kt 8710. Disposal income taxed (Tax Code arts. 132–133, 353, 355).

## Уставга кабул килиш топшириш далолатномаси шаблон (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic)
Act of acceptance-transfer of goods/property **into** LLC charter fund. Parties: founder (topshirdim) → LLC
director (qabul qildim). Table: №, name, unit, qty, price, sum, total.

## Чикиб кетаётган таъсисчига ... далолатномаси шаблон (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic)
Mirror act: property transferred **out of** charter fund to an exiting founder. Signed by chairman of founders'
general meeting (gives) and exiting founder (receives). Same goods table.

## Учредитель_акт_приема_устав_фонда (docx, Russian; 3 identical copies)
"АКТ приема-передачи имущества, вносимого учредителем в качестве вклада в уставный капитал ООО".
Founder (legal entity, via director acting on basis of …) ↔ LLC (via director). Clauses: 1) per protocol/decision
№ … founder transfers property; 2) value; 3) owned by founder, unencumbered; 4) number of copies. Signatures + МП.

## АКТ ПРИЁМА ПЕРЕДАЧИ 1 (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic, filled example)
Topshirish-qabul qilish dalolatnomasi: founder transfers items (table, e.g. table, cabinets) worth total to form
charter fund. ⚠ Example has amount/words mismatch (30 000 000 vs "уч юз миллион") — always validate
amount-in-words generation.

## Протокол дивиденд (doc, Russian, filled example)
"ПРОТОКОЛ № 2 общего собрания учредителей ООО" — dividend distribution. Date, time, attendees (3 founders),
"100% голосов. Кворум имеется", chairman, secretary. Agenda: 1) direct retained profit for Q1 to dividends and
pay dividend tax 10%; 2) distribute cash dividends among founders. "Слушали / Постановили / Голосовали:
единогласно". Amounts with words in сум + тийин. Founders' signatures + М.П.
⚠ Dividend tax rate in this sample (10%) is outdated; the Uzbek template below uses 5%.

## Протокол_Приказ_на_получ_дивиденд (docx) — ⚠ misnamed
Actually a filled example of the **LLC establishment decision №1 + director order №1** ("ASL IMMUN", 2018),
same structure as "МЧЖ ташкил этиш ягона иштирокчи қарори ва директор буйруғи". Order basis includes the
registration certificate number.

## Дивиденд олиш учун йиғилиш баёни (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic, template; 2 copies)
Founders' general meeting protocol (БАЁНИ №) on distributing quarterly net profit.
- Fields: LLC name, city, date, chairman, secretary, attendees list, year, quarter, net profit (минг сўм),
  amount distributed.
- Narrative: chairman opens → director reports quarterly results → chairman proposes approval of quarterly
  reports & balance, distribution ∝ shares under LLC Law arts. 25 & 30 and charter, withhold **5%** dividend
  income tax at source; bank fees for card/deposit transfers covered by company. Unanimous vote.
- ҚАРОР: 1) accept financial statements; 2) distribute net profit ∝ shares; 3) withhold tax & remit to budget;
  4) bank fees borne by company; table [№, participant, share %, profit share, 5% tax, net]; total; 5) chief
  accountant pays lump-sum or in instalments; 6) execution on director. Signatures of chairman, secretary,
  members.

## БАЁН хадя машина Матиз (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic, filled example package)
Package for gifting a company car (Matiz) to a citizen:
1) Protocol of employees' general meeting (chairman, secretary, attendees; director's speech; chief accountant
   and deputy support; ҚАРОР: gift car to X; chief accountant writes it off balance; responsible person
   deregisters it with state bodies; control retained).
2) Director's order (Буйруқ №) to gift and remove from balance. Basis: meeting decision.
3) Ишончнома (power of attorney) to a person to sign gift documents (passport data); for notary and
   traffic police (ИИБ ЙХХБ РИБ).
4) Маълумотнома (certificate) of the car's balance value as of date — for the notary.

## ХАДЯ ШАРТНОМАСИ (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic, template between legal entities)
Gift (donation) agreement. Header: place, date; Donor (Ҳадя қилувчи) and Donee (Ҳадя олувчи) — legal entity
name, acting on basis of (Устав/Низом/ишончнома), signatory FIO.
Sections: I Subject (property list: name, qty) · II Rights (donee may refuse before transfer; claim damage from
defects; donor may claim real damages if donee refuses after signing; donor may refuse if its property status
seriously deteriorated) · III Term & order of transfer (address; by acceptance act; title documents handed over;
inspection; obvious defects → accept or refuse; refusal in writing; deadline N days) — **Variants**: real estate →
notarization + state registration at donee's cost; vehicles → notarization at donee's cost; common joint
property → written consent of co-owners · IV Title passes on signing acceptance act · V Liability (damage from
defects; lost profit if donor knew and didn't warn; damage calc submitted within N days; paid within N bank days)
· VI Rights/obligations do not pass to successors (variant: do pass) · VII Effective on signing; ends on signing
act · VIII Termination (refusal of either; N days' written notice; variant 8.3 no damages for refusal)
· IX Final (law; negotiations → economic court; 2 copies) · X Requisites (СТИР/TIN, legal address, phone/fax,
settlement account, bank, МФО) · XI Signatures & seals (М.Ў.).
