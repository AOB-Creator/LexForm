# HR / labour documents (Кадрга боғлиқ)

Governing law: Labour Code of RUz (Меҳнат кодекси, ЎзР МК). Note: many templates cite article numbers of the
**old** Labour Code (1995); the new Labour Code took effect 30.04.2023, so article references (e.g. 77–87, 99,
104, 151) must be re-mapped before reuse. Standard employment contract form comes from CabMin Resolution №133
of 11.03.1997, Annex 2.

## Мехнат шартномаси шаблон (docx, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies) — official standard form
"Меҳнат шартномаси (контракт)нинг намунавий шакли" (CabMin №133, annex 2). Numbered clauses:
1 Employer (full name of enterprise, represented by FIO) & Employee (FIO; may be non-citizen) · 2 Position
(structural unit; profession per ЯТММ/unified tariff-qualification handbook, grade, category) · 3 Contract type
(main job / part-time combination ўриндошлик / other) · 4 Term (indefinite; fixed ≤ 5 years; for duration of
specific work) · 5 Start / end dates · 6 Probation (none / period) · 7 Employee duties a–з (+ free text: quality,
norms) · 8 Employer duties a–и · 9 Working-time regime (part-time day/week, reduced hours, hourly, start/end)
· 10 Pay: a) type & amount (sum / ЯТС grade / % of revenue); b) statutory supplements; c) bonuses per collective
agreement · 11 Annual paid leave: basic N working days + additional N days (name) · 12 Other conditions
· Addresses & signatures (employee: date, signature; employer: date, signature, seal).

## Мехнат шарт.янгиси (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies) — company-specific version
LLC (director acting under Charter) ↔ citizen. Main job, fixed term, start/end, probation.
- Cl. 7: early termination of fixed-term contract → penalty (неустойка) up to 30% of average monthly wage
  (old LC art. 104).
- Expanded duties (work plan, quality standards, care of property). 6-day week per internal rules. Pay per
  staffing table + supplements + bonuses.
- Cl. 12 termination grounds list (mutual consent, insufficient qualification, systematic/one gross violation,
  court sentence, hiring-rule breach, medical, death, failed probation, representative commission finding).
- Leave; right of complaint via representative commission; Uzbek language, 2 copies.
- Requisites: employer address, ИНН, МФО, р/с; employee FIO, passport series/№, issue date/issuer, validity,
  registered address, phone, ИНН; acknowledgement of internal rules, job description, safety & fire rules.

## мехнат шартнома (docx; 2 copies) — individual entrepreneur ↔ employee
"Хусусий тадбиркор ва хусусий тадбиркорнинг ходими ўртасидаги МЕҲНАТ ШАРТНОМАСИ №". Place, date.
1 IE (with СТИР/ИНН) = Employer; citizen = Employee · 2 Profession & activity/labour functions · 3 Term (months /
indefinite / for work) · 4 Start & end dates · 5 Employee duties · 6 Employer duties · 7 Regime (start/end time,
week type, day off, leave length) · 8 Pay (amount, form, periodicity) · 9 Other: 9.1 employer pays fixed tax and
insurance contributions for employee; 9.2 other · Addresses & signatures.

## Ишдан бушуш хакида буйрук (docx; 2 copies, filled example)
Order №5 "Меҳнат шартномасини бекор қилиш ҳақида": terminate contract with employee (position) from date;
accounting pays compensation for N unused leave days for working year period. Basis: employee's application,
LC arts. 99, 151 (old). Signed by head; HR inspector; "Буйруқ билан танишдим" (acknowledged) + employee.

## Буйрук Директор бушатиш (docx; 2 copies)
Two orders signed by the **founder**: №1 "Ишдан бўшатиш ҳақида" — director released at own wish from date
(basis: application; acknowledgement signature/date). №2 "Мажбуриятларни юклаш ҳақида" — deputy director
appointed acting director from same date.

## Буйрук штат жадвали (docx; 2 copies)
Order "Штат жадвалини тасдиқлаш тўғрисида": for year N set X staff units, monthly payroll fund Y сум; units and
salaries may change per finances; control retained by head.

## Штат_жадвали_3 (docx; 2 copies, filled)
Staffing table (ШТАТ ЖАДВАЛИ) — annex to Order №3 of year. Header: org name, effective year, number of units,
monthly payroll fund. Table: №, profession (position), number of units, salary, sum. Signed by director & chief
accountant.

## Буйрук инвентаризация (doc; 2 copies)
Order on inventory of fixed assets, inventory (ТМЗ) and receivables/payables. Basis: Law "On Accounting" art. 16,
NAS №19 "Inventory organization". Creates commission (chairman + members with positions), N days to complete;
chairman summarizes results in N days; control on X. Approval block: Киритилди (submitted), Келишилди (agreed),
Таништирилди (acknowledged) — position + FIO lines.

## Буйруклар шаблон рус тилида / Все приказы (docx, Russian; identical, 3 copies) — HR orders library
~45 ready-made "ПРИКАЗ" templates. Common shape: title → (preamble "В связи с …") → ПРИКАЗЫВАЮ / ПРИНЯТЬ /
ПРЕДОСТАВИТЬ / ПЕРЕВЕСТИ / ПРЕКРАТИТЬ → body → "Основание(я)" → "Директор: ___" → "С приказом ознакомлен(а): ___"
→ "Дата". Article refs are **old Labour Code (ТК 1995)**.
- Hiring: standard (position, dept, from date, salary per staffing table, personnel № "табельный номер"; basis
  employment contract); temporary replacement with hourly rate; fixed period with double pay for weekends/holidays
  (or day off + single pay); replacement of employee on maternity/childcare leave with full material liability
  (bases: contract, chief accountant's consent, material liability agreement).
- Working time: weekend work (union committee consent; arts. 220, 228, 245; double pay); duty (дежурство) with
  time off within 10 days; overtime (≤2h per employee; double pay art. 157; arts. 124–125).
- Leave: annual leave (working year period, N working days, return date; bases application + leave schedule);
  recall from leave (arts. 145, 147); leave partly replaced by money (art. 151); unpaid leave (art. 150);
  leave for mother of two children <12 → 24 + 3 days (art. 232).
- Discipline: fine 50% of average monthly wage for systematic violations; deduction of material damage;
  bonus deprivation (union-agreed).
- Changes: workplace change (art. 91); permanent transfer (art. 92); transfer for health reasons with 2 weeks'
  average wage kept (art. 218); temporary lighter work (art. 218); temporary transfer to replace absent employee
  (art. 93); combination of posts (совмещение) paid per act.
- Termination (each: full settlement + compensation for unused leave days): failed probation (art. 87);
  refusal of new conditions (art. 89 p.4, severance ≥ 1 avg monthly wage art. 109, guarantees art. 67);
  mutual agreement (art. 97 p.1); own wish without/with good reason (art. 99); redundancy (art. 100 p.1 part 2,
  union consent, severance art. 109); systematic violations (art. 100 p.3; 3 days' compensation instead of notice
  art. 102); one gross violation (art. 100 p.4); part-timer replaced by full-timer (art. 100 p.5); change of owner
  (art. 100 p.6); expiry of fixed term (art. 97 p.3); return of absent employee (art. 105); military conscription
  (art. 106); reinstatement of previous employee by court (art. 106 p.2); court sentence (art. 106 p.3); death
  (art. 106 p.5, funeral allowance art. 288); hiring-rules violation (relatives in state enterprise, art. 79/106
  p.4); parents' demand for minor (art. 106 p.6, art. 247); refusal to take part in competitive re-election
  (art. 97 p.6).
- Other: working-condition change for disabled employee (36 h/week; arts. 88, 90, 116, 135); position rename
  (art. 89); correction of dismissal date due to late work-book issue (art. 108; forced absence pay art. 275);
  business trip (report within 3 days); delegation of director's powers during absence; full material liability
  agreements (per collective agreement clause); compensation for death of breadwinner (6 average annual
  earnings lump sum within a month; moral damage); reinstatement by court decision (arts. 111–112); staff
  reduction (2 months' personal notice, inform district employment center; arts. 100, 102); bonuses (quarterly
  0.5 of salary; holiday bonus with full/partial deprivation, art. 180); part-time work during childcare leave
  (≤6 h/day, keep allowance until child is 2; art. 234); re-conclusion after competitive election (≤5 years).

## Жамоа шартномаси (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies) — collective agreement model (~180 KB)
Recommended model text ("макет"); mandatory statutory norms were marked in red in the original. Title page:
approved by general meeting (conference) of labour collective, protocol №; enterprise; term (years); signed by
employer and trade-union committee chairman + representatives of each side.
Sections: I General (parties; effective from/to, continues until replaced; applies to all employees incl. new
hires, sometimes family members, retirees, union staff) · II Production & economic activity · III Employment
contract, employment guarantees · IV Working time & rest (≤36 h for harmful conditions; cumulative accounting;
cold-season heating breaks; additional leave categories) · V Pay & guaranteed payments (single tariff scale ЕТС;
bonuses regulation; reimbursement for use of employee's property per Tax Code art. 144; supplements and
coefficients; student interns per CabMin №40 of 11.02.2009; annual bonus) · VI Labour discipline · VII Material
liability (full liability agreements mandatory for cashier per MoJ reg. №2687; brigade liability) · VIII Labour
protection (annual agreement per joint resolution of 03.09.2012; PPE per LC art. 217; free milk; 400 g soap/month;
medical checks per MoH order №200 of 10.07.2012) · IX Social insurance & benefits · X Cultural, sports, health
· XI Women & family-duty guarantees · XII Youth guarantees · XIII Labour disputes (commission regulation) · XIV
Social partnership, trade-union guarantees · XV Control of performance (published within 1 month) · XVI Liability
for breach.
Recommended list of **39 annexes**: labour-protection agreement; training regulation; home-working regulation;
cumulative time-accounting list; cold-season breaks; additional-leave lists (harmful, special, seniority,
managers, climate); pay regulation; bonus regulation; property-use compensation; mobility/shift/seniority/harmful
supplements; climate supplements; reduced piece rates; mentors' pay; interns' pay; annual bonus regulation;
full/brigade material liability lists; harmful works list; free milk, soap, PPE lists; medical-check list; leave
supplement regulation; labour-disputes commission regulation and its facilities; material aid; interest-free
housing loans; cultural/sports budget; union facilities; health fund.

## Мансаб йурикномаси Рахбар ва Хисобчи (rtf, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies) — job descriptions
Two job descriptions: (1) Head of organization (9 duties: overall management per law; coordinate units; control
distribution of duties; execute tasks of higher bodies & khokimiyat; staffing & training; working conditions;
represent org & sign contracts; manage production, income/expense accounting; collective agreement).
"Билиши керак" (must know) block; qualification: higher/incomplete higher, ≥5 years' experience.
(2) Chief accountant (9 duties: accounting & reporting per law; rational forms; cash/inventory/fixed-asset
accounting; prices, payroll, taxes & social payments; efficiency; distribute duties in accounting; submit reports;
comply with national & international standards). Qualification: higher economic/financial/engineering-economic,
≥4 years.

## образец-объективка-узб (doc, Uzbek Cyrillic; 2 copies) — "Маълумотнома" (objektivka)
Official personnel reference (government-style CV). Fields: FIO; current position since date; birth date &
place; nationality; party membership; education & institution (year); specialty; academic degree & title; foreign
languages; military/special rank; state awards; elected-body membership; work history (years – position).
Formatting rules: Times New Roman 11, .doc; margins top 1.5, bottom 1, right 1, left 2 cm; no abbreviations;
verify FIO by passport, work history by work book, education by diploma; only fluent languages; electronic file
named by full FIO in Cyrillic (у,к,г,х instead of ў,қ,ғ,ҳ). Second part: table of **close relatives**
(relation, FIO incl. maiden name, birth year & place, workplace & position, residence) with right/wrong
formatting examples (no abbreviations; deceased → "YYYY йил вафот этган (former position)"; retired →
"Пенсияда (former position)").

## Резюме шаблон Ўзбекча, Русча, Инглизча (doc) — trilingual CV
Same CV form in Russian, Uzbek Cyrillic and English: position applied; personal data (FIO, DOB dd/mm/yyyy, sex,
marital status, registration & present address, home/mobile phones, e-mail); education (level; table from/till,
institution, specialty, degree); additional training (period, course, certificate); work experience (period,
organization, position, main responsibilities).

## бланка пенс справка 2021г (docx, Russian; 2 copies) — salary certificate for pension
"Справка о заработке" — Annex №12 to Regulation on state pensions. Issued to FIO. Table 1: months Jan–Dec ×
(salary incl. other payments, insurance contributions accrued) for up to 5 years; total. Table 2: monthly/quarterly
bonuses & annual rewards (month/year paid, amount, period, indicators). Basis documents (payroll books, ledgers).
For district department of the off-budget Pension Fund under MinFin. Signed by head and chief accountant, М.П.
Note: corrections in payroll books must be indicated.

## Маълумотнома (объективка)

Standard personal reference submitted for appointments. Template: `personal-reference`.

- **Page 1 — Маълумотнома:** 3×4 colour photo (taken within the last 3 months, formal wear); full name; "since <date>:" + current employer and position; paired fields — year/place of birth, nationality/party membership, education/graduated (year, institution, full-time/part-time), speciality, academic degree/title, foreign languages/military (special) rank; state awards; deputy or elected-body membership; **МЕҲНАТ ФАОЛИЯТИ** — one line per period: "2004-2008 йй. - …", current post as "2021 й. - ҳ.в. - …".
- **Page 2 — "<Исм Отасининг исми Фамилияси>нинг яқин қариндошлари ҳақида МАЪЛУМОТ":** table with columns Қариндошлиги · Ф.И.Ш. · Туғилган йили ва жойи · Иш жойи ва лавозими · Турар жойи.
- **Rules (from the form's notes):** Times New Roman 11, MS Word .doc; margins top 1.5 cm, bottom 1 cm, right 1 cm, left 2 cm; no abbreviations; name checked against the passport, work history against the work record book, education against the diploma; only fluently spoken languages; maiden name in brackets — "Раҳимова (Назарова) Дилноза"; deceased: "1992 йил вафот этган (former post)", pensioners: "Пенсияда (former post)" without "илгари"/"бўлган"; full region/district/street in addresses; electronic file named with the full name in Cyrillic, using у, к, г, х instead of ў, қ, ғ, ҳ (e.g. "Рахимов Жасур Ахмадович.doc").
